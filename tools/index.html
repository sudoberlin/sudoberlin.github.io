My 16-week plan for robotics software, from C++ and ROS 2 through MoveIt, Nav2, perception and robot learning to a full capstone. Each week has tasks and a deliverable to tick off, with notes and progress saved in the browser.

Paper Companion

Reading research papers · source

Paste an arXiv link or upload a PDF. It splits the paper into its real sections, explains each one at the level I pick, and gives every section its own chat that has read that exact text. Each explanation ends with two questions to check I actually understood it.
